# Two hosts

Date line `<!-- YYYY-MM-DD -->` or `<!-- YYYY-MM-DD two-host -->`.

| speaker | role | voice en / es |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| HOST_A | asks, reacts, sums up | af_bella / ef_dora |
| HOST_B | explains | am_michael / em_alex |

- HOST_B has fifty five to eighty percent of the words (aim for seventy).
- Never two turns in a row by the same host.
- HOST_A's first turn is the welcome and the three things. HOST_A gives the recap and the
  one-sentence sign-off.

## Two devices that make it sound like a conversation

- *Mid-sentence handoff.* HOST_A leaves a sentence unfinished, with no final punctuation,
  and HOST_B finishes it. The renderer joins them with a short gap.
- *Let the listener name it.* When HOST_B builds a picture, HOST_A says the word for it.

Put the two devices on different pairs of turns.
