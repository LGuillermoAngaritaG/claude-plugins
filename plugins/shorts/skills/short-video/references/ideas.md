# The ideas file

`src/videos/<series>/ideas.md` is the one thing the user approves before a batch is built. It is short enough to
read in two minutes and concrete enough that a builder can make each Short from it without asking.

```markdown
# <series>: ideas

<n> Shorts for <channel or topic>, <date>. Looks: <n> explainer, <n> sketch, <n> story. Clips: <n> (about <m> GPU
minutes) or none.

## 1. <Title>

- **Look:** explainer | sketch | story, and why in one line
- **Length:** about <n> s (45 s to 2.5 min), and why in a few words: as short as the idea allows, as long as it
  needs to be explained well
- **Question it answers:** the one thing the viewer knows at the end
- **Hook (line 1):** "<the exact first line>"
- **First two seconds:** what fills the upright frame and how it moves
- **Payoff:** the answer, in one sentence
- **Last line:** "<the exact last line>" (a channel explainer asks a question for the comments)
- **Terms to explain:** at most two, each with its plain meaning
- **Pictures and motion:** the 3D model, Codex pictures, drawings, AI clips (how many) and real footage (each piece:
  stock, or whose trailer, gameplay or video, and why the Short may show it), in order
- **Riskiest claim:** "<the claim>", source: <link>
```

One block per Short, numbered, so the user can answer "1 and 3 yes, 2 change the hook, replace 4".
