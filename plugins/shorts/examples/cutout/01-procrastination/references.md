# Sources (all checked for the channel's week 44 procrastination videos)

- Steel, "The nature of procrastination: a meta-analytic and theoretical review", Psychological Bulletin 133(1), 2007.
  Definition; 691 correlations; task aversiveness a strong, consistent predictor (correlational).
  https://ucalgary.scholaris.ca/handle/1880/47914
- Sirois & Pychyl, "Procrastination and the priority of short-term mood regulation", Social and Personality
  Psychology Compass 7(2), 2013. An argument from a review: said as "some researchers argue".
  https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/91793/
- Ghibellini & Meier, Humanities and Social Sciences Communications 12, 2025: meta-analysis, people tend to go back
  to an interrupted task (Ovsiankina effect). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05000-w
- Milyavskaya & Inzlicht, Social Psychological and Personality Science 8(6), 2017: goal attainment went with fewer
  temptations; effortful self-control was unrelated to it (correlational). https://doi.org/10.1177/1948550616679237
- Wohl, Pychyl & Bennett, Personality and Individual Differences 48(7), 2010: 119 first-year students; self-forgiveness
  after the first exam, less procrastination before the second (a survey study, not an experiment).
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2010.01.029
