# The outline

In a channel's week the user approves outlines, not scripts. An outline is short enough to read seven at once and
says everything the user would change before the work starts. One per video, all of a week in one file,
`src/videos/<week series>/outlines.md`, numbered so the user can answer by number.

```markdown
# <Channel>, week <nn> (<first day> to <last day>)

## 1. <Title>
**Viewer:** who it is for, what they know already. **Length:** about <n> minutes.
**The question:** one sentence. **The payoff:** the last thing the viewer understands.
**First line:** the hook as it will be said, a question to the viewer ("Did you know ...?", "What happens if you ...?").
**Map:** the line that says what the video covers and the part worth staying for.
**Comment question:** the question the video ends on, about the viewer's own life or opinion.

| Chapter | Its question | Medium | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | 3D cutaway of ... / worked example / AI clips of ... / Codex pictures | 2 |

**Riskiest claims:** the three a specialist would check first, each with its source.
- "<claim>" ([source](https://...))
**Clips:** <n> clips (about one a minute, `media.md`), what they show in a few words, about <minutes> of GPU.
**Short runs:** the one-minute stretches the channel asks for (`shorts`), in a few words each, with their word count
(150 to 190 each; a calm voice reads about 150 words a minute).

## 2. ...
```

Rules:

- Each title answers or asks what the video is about, with no promise the script cannot keep (no "cure", no "the
  secret").
- Every riskiest claim already has a source you opened. A topic whose main claim you cannot source is replaced
  before the outlines go to the user.
- No two outlines of the week, or of the channel's earlier weeks, answer the same question.
